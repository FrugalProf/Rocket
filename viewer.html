If you fill in the field above, the QR code will point to this page (with the model URL attached), so scanning it always opens the correct preview and AR button. Leave it blank and the QR will point straight at the .glb file instead — this can work but behaves inconsistently across phones, since there's no page to run the AR handoff logic.
iOS note: Apple's AR Quick Look expects a .usdz file, not .glb. When this page's AR button is tapped on an iPhone, model-viewer tries to auto-generate a .usdz on the fly, but this only works reliably for simple models and isn't guaranteed. If AR fails specifically on iPhone but works on Android, exporting a .usdz version alongside your .glb (many 3D tools can do this) and hosting both is the more dependable fix.
